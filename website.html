Questions

Common questions

Does it need internet?

No. Everything works offline. Internet is only used to send marks to teachers and update the rankings.

Is it free?

Yes. No adverts, no payments and no login.

I got a new phone. Is my progress lost?

No. In the old app, open Progress → Backup and restore code and write down your code. On the new phone choose "I have a restore code".

What information is saved?

Your name, school, class and study progress, so your teachers can help you. Read the privacy policy.

Does it work on iPhone or computer?

Yes. On iPhone, open the iPhone app in Safari and tap Share, then "Add to Home Screen". There is also a version for Windows computers — ask your teacher.
